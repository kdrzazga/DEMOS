Designed by Chuck Peddle and Leonard Tramiel
and first appearing on the Commodore PET in 1977,
it partially overlaps with ASCII but adds a distinctive
library of geometric and semi-graphic
symbols: lines, corners, circles, checkerboards,
and card-suit shapes.

On the Commodore 64 specifically, those
 symbols got a thicker stroke weight,
 likely to stay legible on a TV
 screen. This is the character set
 most people associate with the term
 "PETSCII," and it's the one this
 study focuses on.
