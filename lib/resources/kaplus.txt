After a long break, K&A Plus returns with its milestone Issue #30,
celebrating everything we love about the Commodore retro scene.
The main theme of this anniversary edition is multiplayer and cooperative gaming –
from classic experiences to modern homebrew titles that prove retro gaming is still
best enjoyed together.



Inside, you’ll find reviews of the latest releases, including Impossible Mission III,
Traps N’Gemstones, Orbital Mission, and Terra Cresta 2026, alongside a rich selection
of technical articles, programming tutorials, interviews,
and features from across the Commodore community. Continue your journey through 6502 Assembly – Part VII, build a Scientific Calculator on the C64 in BASIC, discover Amiga in the Hands of the Famous, and read an exclusive interview with Raistlin of Genesis Project, plus much more.



The new issue is available now in both printed and digital editions in our store



Following the success of the original Tantooman, we are proud to present Tantooman Volume Two
a brand-new collection of 120 additional levels for the Commodore 64 and Commodore 128.
Once again, you take control of the little hero TANTOOMAN, navigating colourful mazes, painting every corridor, avoiding deadly enemies, and racing against the relentless countdown clock.

As this is an expansion to Volume One, the base version of the product includes only
the floppy disk and a 2-page manual. This is a courtesy to players who already own Tantooman Volume 1,
allowing them to purchase just the new floppy disk, which fits perfectly into their existing box.
Customers who do not yet own Tantooman should additionally purchase the box, available as an option.
Together, these two components form the complete boxed edition of the game.

Get ready for a fruity adventure in Hipofruta, a colourful and addictive platform game for the Commodore 64.
 Take control of a hungry hippopotamus as you leap through 20 action-packed levels,
collecting delicious fruit while avoiding spiders, snakes, bats, and other dangerous creatures lurking
along the way. Packed with secrets, switches, bonus items, and a showdown against the fearsome Great Lemon,
Hipofruta delivers classic arcade fun with charm and personality. The game is available separately
on both 5.25″ floppy disk and cassette tape, making it a perfect addition to any C64 collection.
